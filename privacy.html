Security & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information Collection

We collect essential personal information when you create an account, place an order, or interact with our platform. This includes your name, email address, shipping details, and transaction histories stored securely in our Oracle Database backend.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for processing orders, managing customer accounts, providing real-time inventory updates, and ensuring high-speed transactional reliability across our Node.js middleware architecture.

3. Data Protection & Security

We enforce strict security protocols and enterprise-grade relational database encryption to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your personal information.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Modern Store uses browser local storage and essential session tokens to remember cart items and keep you logged in seamlessly as you navigate between pages.
